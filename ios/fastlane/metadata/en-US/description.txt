Write a story with strangers, one blind turn at a time.

Cadavre Exquisite brings the classic surrealist game "Exquisite Corpse" to your phone. Every story is written by four different authors, and each one sees only the last five words of what came before. Nobody knows where the story is going until it's finished, and then everyone gets to read the whole unexpected, funny, sometimes poetic result.

HOW IT WORKS
Every story has four parts, each written by a different player:
• Introduction: open a brand-new story from a blank page.
• Development (1/2): continue it, seeing only the last five words of the introduction.
• Development (2/2): push the plot further, again with just a five-word hint.
• Epilogue: bring the story to an ending, whatever it turned out to be.

Once the epilogue is written, the full story is revealed in the Complete tab, with every part shown in order next to its author.

END WITH A HOOK
Since the next player sees only your last five words, how you finish your part matters. Leave a cliffhanger, a strange image or a half-finished thought, and see what someone else does with it.

FEATURES

AI-generated story titles
Every finished story gets its own title, generated from what the four authors wrote, so each tale in the archive has a name worth clicking.

Language rooms
Write and read stories in Italian, English, Spanish, French or German. Every story belongs to a language room, so it's written entirely by people who share that language. The app starts you in the room that matches your device language, and you can switch rooms at any time.

Private rooms for friends
Create a private room with a name and password and share it with your friends, your class or your writing group. Stories written there stay in the room, so you can play the game together.

Fair, turn-based writing
Only one person writes a story at a time. While you're writing, the story is locked for everyone else and shown as "being written", so no two players can write the same part. You can't write two parts in a row either, so every story really has several authors.

Nickname or anonymous
Pick a nickname to appear next to your parts in completed stories, or stay anonymous. It's up to you.

Your stories at a glance
Stories you've contributed to are labelled, so you can see which ones are still waiting for other players and come back once they're complete.

Read the archive
Browse every completed story in your room and read it from start to finish, part by part.

Quick sign-in
Sign in with your email or continue with Google.

WHO IT'S FOR
• Writers looking for an unusual creative warm-up.
• Friends who want a quirky game to play together from anywhere.
• Teachers and students exploring creative writing in a playful way.
• Anyone who enjoys absurd, surprising stories.

Start a story, continue someone else's, and see what you write together.
