Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to grillo.luca@gmail.com. Please mention your device and app version if you're reporting a problem.

Why can I see only five words of the story?

That's the game. Each player sees only the end of the previous part, and the whole story is revealed once all four parts are written.

Why can't I continue a story?

Someone else may be writing it right now: only one person can write a story at a time. You also can't write two parts in a row, so wait for another player to take the next turn.

How do I play with friends only?

Open the menu, choose Private room and create a room with a name and password. Share them with your friends so they can join.

How do I change the name shown in stories?

Go to Profile and set a nickname. Without one, you appear as the first two letters of your email.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. Your account and nickname are deleted, and the parts you wrote stay in their stories as Anonymous. If you can't access the app, email grillo.luca@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Email grillo.luca@gmail.com with the story title and what's wrong with it.
