Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to grillo.luca@gmail.com. Please mention your device and app version if you're reporting a problem.

Why can I see only five words of the story?

That's the game. Each player sees only the end of the previous part, plus up to four keywords that hint at the story so far. The whole story is revealed once all four parts are written.

Why can't I continue a story?

Someone else may be writing it right now: only one person can write a story at a time. You also can't write two parts in a row, so wait for another player to take the next turn. If someone opens a story and doesn't finish, it's freed for others after an hour.

How do I play with friends only?

Go to Profile, open Private room in the Room section and create a room with a name and password. Share them with your friends so they can join. In the same section you can also switch language room.

How do I choose the name shown in stories?

Go to Profile and set a nickname. You can choose it only once, so pick carefully. Without one, you appear as the first two letters of your email.

How do I get notified when someone continues my story?

Accept the notifications when the app asks, or turn them on in Profile under Notifications. If they're blocked in your phone's settings, turn them on there.

Why are some stories locked?

Finished stories with adult content are marked automatically and shown as locked tiles. Tap one to confirm your age and read it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. Your account and nickname are deleted, and the parts you wrote stay in their stories as Anonymous. If you can't access the app, email grillo.luca@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Tap the ⋮ menu next to a part (in a finished story, or above the last words when you're writing) and choose Report this part. We review every report within 24 hours, remove content that breaks the rules and close the accounts of the players who wrote it. You can also email grillo.luca@gmail.com.

How do I block a player?

From the same ⋮ menu, choose Block. You won't see stories with parts written by that player anymore. To undo it, go to Profile and tap Unblock under Blocked players.
