Write a story with strangers, one blind turn at a time.

Cadavre Exquisite brings the surrealist game "Exquisite Corpse" to your phone. Every story is written by four different players, and each one sees only the last five words of what came before. When the story is finished, everyone gets to read the whole thing.

Get it on Google Play

How it works

  1. Introduction: someone opens a new story from a blank page.
  2. Development (1/2): the next player continues it, seeing only the last five words.
  3. Development (2/2): another player pushes the plot further.
  4. Epilogue: a fourth player writes the ending.

Then the full story is revealed, with each part next to its author.

Features

  • AI-generated titles for every finished story
  • Language rooms: Italian, English, Spanish, French and German
  • Private rooms with a password, for friends, classes and writing groups
  • One writer at a time, and never two parts in a row
  • Appear with a nickname, or stay anonymous
